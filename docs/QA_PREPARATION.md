1. Why does this project study expressed responsibility/verification rather than actual workplace productivity? Public comments are discourse evidence, not observation of work or code quality.
2. Why these five threads? Answer from the group's real selection record; the files alone do not establish a systematic sampling procedure.
3. What changed from 313 to 278? Exactly 30 dead and 5 deleted records; preserved shared fields are unchanged. The later annotation file adds only verification_specific; its 13 original columns match the frozen clean data.

5. Why can't RQ1 dictionary hits replace verification_specific? Different operationalisations. The supplied labels are preserved and the recovered 123-ID list reproduces them. The group reports an Excel-code step; its exact expression remains unavailable. Explain the intended checking/testing definition and the unresolved quoted, implicit and ambiguous examples without claiming validated manual coding.
6. Why p = 0.8008? The table recalculated from 278 supplied rows is [[86, 69], [71, 52]], with explicit Yates correction. A large p-value does not prove no association/effect, equivalence, independence or causality. The descriptive by-thread table reveals composition; it does not repair the dependence assumption or add a new test.
7. Are comments independent? Not established; repeated authors and conversations threaten the model. Expected counts>5 addresses a different assumption.
8. How is TF-IDF formed? Log within-document counts times inverse document frequency, then L2normalisation; cosine compares profiles.
9. Why does clustering not prove themes? Any cut gives groups; poor silhouette, tiny clusters and heterogeneous examples can undermine semantic interpretation.
10. Why compare linkages? To check sensitivity, not to choose desired findings. Ward uses Euclidean distances here.
11. Why144 graph authors rather than186? Only authors participating in matched retained between-author edges become nodes; roots and missing parents are excluded.
12. Why198 events but193 edges? Repeated ordered author pairs are collapsed; frequency is stored separately.
13. What distinguishes in-degree and betweenness? Distinct incoming authors versus directed shortest-path positions. Neither proves expertise/agreement/influence.
14. How do clusters join to replies? Through comment_id; receiving content is the parent comment. Authors can have multiple content memberships.
15. How do the archived collector/cleaner differ from the integrated scripts? They preserve group-repository evidence and historical paths; the integrated functions use stable shared paths, and optional recollection writes a new snapshot. Commit time is not collection time.
16. How are authorship details kept consistent? Report and poster read the same member/SID/contribution CSV, with percentages summing to 100; the group confirms actual contributions.
17. What would you improve with more time? Documented sampling and collection completeness, validated annotations, better unit definitions and robust measurement before more complex models.
