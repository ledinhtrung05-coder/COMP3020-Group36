## Completed AI-SENS-v1 sensitivity results

The masked full-text reading covers 278 comments: 128 TRUE, 145 FALSE and 5 AMBIGUOUS. Among 273 determinate cases, 14 differ from supplied labels (11 supplied FALSE to AI TRUE; 3 supplied TRUE to AI FALSE). Prior context exposed some labelled examples; this is not independent blinded validation. Exact excerpts and rationales are preserved, with the CSV frozen before outcomes were joined.

The determinate-only contrast is +1.083 percentage points (57/128 versus 63/145), compared with the original -2.240 points. Uniform ambiguous-as-FALSE and ambiguous-as-TRUE scenarios give +1.865 and +0.161 points. These are descriptive sensitivity results, not validation, error rates, bounds or extra significance tests. The directional conclusion depends on measurement choices as well as author weighting. `outputs/tables/rq2_annotation_sensitivity.csv` contains the calculations.
